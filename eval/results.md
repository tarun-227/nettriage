# Accuracy

Generated by `eval/run_eval.py`. Detector weights were set on the dev seeds (1-3) and the curated samples only. The holdout seeds (101-105) are never used for tuning: they are generated and scored after development (latest run: after the MTU detector and incident correlation were added).

**How to read these numbers.** Every capture here comes from our own seeded office simulator (`samples/generate/`), and the same person wrote the simulator and the detectors. The scores show that each detector does what it was designed to do. They also show that it stays stable across random seeds, scenario sizes and background noise, and that it does not fire on the benign look-alikes we modelled. They are **not** a measurement of accuracy on real networks, which have behaviour we did not model. Real-tool captures from an isolated lab are the next test.
