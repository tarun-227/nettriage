# Accuracy

Generated by `eval/run_eval.py`. Detector weights were set on the dev seeds (1-3) only. The holdout seeds (101-105) were generated once and evaluated once, after development.

**How to read these numbers.** Every capture here comes from our own seeded office simulator (`samples/generate/`), and the same team wrote the simulator and the detectors. The scores show that each detector does what it was designed to do. They also show that it stays stable across random seeds, scenario sizes and background noise, and that it does not fire on the benign look-alikes we modelled. They are **not** a measurement of accuracy on real networks, which have behaviour we did not model. Real-tool captures from an isolated lab are the next test.
